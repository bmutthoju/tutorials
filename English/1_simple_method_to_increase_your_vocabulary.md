# 1 Simple Method to Increase Your Vocabulary #
1. 3R method
	1. Read - Record - Review

## Read ##
1. Read for 1 hour a day (at leat 1/2 hour)
	1. Most benefit
2. Read what is interesting
	1. Sports, movies, cars, fitness, electronics, etc.
3. Find materials to read
	1. Magazines
	2. Novels
	3. Short stories
	4. Biographies
	5. Online articles
	6. Wikipedia
	7. ...
4. Don't look up every difficult word in a dictionary
	1. It is boring and distracting
5. Focus on overall idea of what you are reading
6. For any difficult word
	1. Try to guess the meaning
	2. If you cannot, mark them or write them down

## Record ##
1. Collect new words
2. Go back to text you read and find words to learn
	1. Two types of words
		1. Unknown words - words you don't know
		2. Inactive words - words you have seen before but are not sure how to use
3. Aim to collect 5-10 new words per day
	1. In a notebook
	2. On your computer (in Word/Excel)
	3. On your phone (using an app)
4. Start with the first word - look it up in a dictionary
	1. Cambridge
	2. Oxford
	3. Merrian-Webster
5. Format

```
<word> | <part-of-speech> | <pronunciation>
--
<definition>
--
1. <example-sentence-1>
2. <example-sentence-2>
--
Ref: <where-I-learnt-the-word>
--
Other forms:
<noun-form>
<verb-form>
<adjective-form>
<adverb-form>
--
Draw a picture <helps recall the meaning>
```

## Review ##
1. The challenge is to bring new workds into your active vocabulary
	1. You can only achieve this by reviewing them regularly.
		1. Pick one day of the week as your review day.
		2. Start by reviewing words from that week.
		3. Then move on to older vocabulary.
2. Review techniques:
	1. Test yourself
		1. Hide the definition and try to remember the meaning
	2. Ask a friend or family member to test you.
	3. Use flashcards
		1. Index card
			1. Word on one side
			2. Definition on the other side
	4. Use flashcard apps
		1. It will test you
	5. Look at the notes
		1. Engage with the word
		2. Try to use it actively in own speech and writing.c
