3. Iteration & Troubleshooting Mindset
	1. To excel with AI, you need a mindset of iteration and troubleshooting.
	2. Review your output (reflect on what worked and what didn't), adjust what didn't work, and keep tweaking your prompts.
	3. The more you experiement and iterate, the better the results will be.
	4. Practice will help you get closer to what you're looking for.
		1. More we iterate, the better we get.
4. **Modular & Reusable Prompts**
	1. Modular prompts are templates you can reuse for tasks that share similar patterns.
	2. Instead of writing a new prompt every time, you can plug in specific details for different tasks
	3. Over time, you'll build a library of go-to prompts for different situations. It's a formula for faster, smarter work.
	4. Example prompt:
		1. "Act as a [role]. Analyze this [example input], and respond in [format] for [context]."
5. **Chat Memory Awareness**
	1. ChatGPT remembers what you discuss within a thread but doesn't retain context across different threads unless memory features are enabled.
	2. This means prompts will behave differently depending on the converstaion's history.
	3. If your output seems off, try starting a fresh thread for clearer results.
6. **Cleaning Data with Prompts**
	1. You can use AI to clean up messy data like credit card statements, meeting notes, or survey data
	2. Ask ChatGPT to filter out irrelevant information, then prompt to work with the remaining data
	3. Credit card statement example:
		1. "Remove any line items not related to travel. Keep only flights, hotels, rental cars, and public transport"
		2. "Add up all the expenses and summarize them by category." (reduction)
	4. Can be used for any complex document which needs refinement and cleaning first
5. **Prompt Priming**
	1. This means preparing a thread with as much relevant context as possible, before you send the original prompt
	2. Whether it's text, a PDF, or a link, the more detail you provide, the better the AI's response will be
	3. Context is key to getting the results you want. The more information you feed into your thread, the more specific and accurate the output
		1. It should be specific data leading towards a desired output, we might be adding extra noise (like the opposite of cleaning data)
	4. Techniques for priming:
		1. **Put written context into the initial input**
			1. Example: Copy and paste background information from Wikipedia
			2. **Tell ChatGPT that we are dropping data without any instructions to act on.**
				1. Example: Before we proceed with instructions, I will provide Wimbledon's 2024 match data
			3. **Tell ChatGPT to only reference the data we input**
				1. Example: Using only the data provided in this thread from Wimbledon 2024 match data, perdict who will win Wimbledon 2025. Don't research the web for extra data.
		2. **Upload reference material**
			1. Screenshots are also good
			2. Give instruction that we are priming ChatGPT:
				1. Example: I will be providing a few inputs to prime our conversation. First here's 2 screenshots of Amazon's Best Selling products in different categories.
			3. Give additional instructions if required 
				1. Example: Giving additional information about best selling products
					1. Now I will provide product descriptions from 3 Best Selling items from different categories. We are still preparing the conversation before acting on my instructions.
						1. ChatGPT still analyses the content and gives insights and the next steps
			4. Prompt ChatGPT with instructions
				1. Example: Now analyze and propose what do they all have in common that have made them successful which I can incorporate in my own Amazon products.
			5. Output: Analysis of multiple aspects + cheatsheet with listing strategy

### Advanced Prompting Frameworks ###
1. More advanced frameworks that breakdown big more complex ideas, improve clarity, and guide the AI through complex thinking tasks

3. **Prompt Chaining**
	1. This framework breaks a task into smaller steps, using the output from one prompt as input for the next
	2. For Sam's product launch, he can start by asking ChatGPT to summarize key steps, then build on that with more detailed tasks like constructing a timeline and writing marketing strategies
	3. Each step builds on the last for a well-structured and coherent plan
	4. This framework gives users more control over each piece of the task
	5. Steps:
		1. Lane 1:
			1. Identify audience segments
				1. Identify key steps 
		2. Lane 2:
			1. Task summary
				1. Task breakdown 
			2. Define a brand voice
				1. Craft a timeline
		4. Lane 3:
			1. Task summary
				1. Task breakdown
			2. Task summary
				1. Task breakdown
			3. Construct a marketing plan
				1. Develop marketing strategy
	6. Purpose
		1. Turn big ideas into easy, manageable actions
		2. Best use cases
			1. Master complex projects with a clear, step-by-step flow
4. **Each step builds on the last step**
	1. We are guiding the AI while keeping the task on track and avoiding the overwhelm of dumping everything into one massive, messy prompt
	2. Example:
		1. Select web-search
		2. Prompt: Summarize the key steps involved in a product launch campaign.
		3. Prompt: Based on the summary, construct a campaign timeline for launching the smart home security cameras (adding prompts to expand further on elements that will be critical for the final output)
		4. Write a marketing strategy for promoting the product through social media channels (work with main aspects of product launch - since the thread has context)
		5. Write a final product launch announcement that includes the timeline and marketing strategies (work with final elements of product launch)
	3. We break down a large task into smaller focussed pieces
		1. Each step is refined before moving to the next
	4. Downside:
		1. Can take more time
			1. Each part of the plan is created sequentially
	5. Benefit:
		1. Each piece is carefully tailored and revised
			1. Best outputs are generated if we put in our creative energy into the inputs
5. **Chain of Thought Reasoning**
	1. This approach involves asking AI to think through a task step-by-step, making sure each part is well-reasoned
		1. Asking AI to breakdown its thinking step-by-step
			1. Useful if quality or factual outputs are very important (we want the AI to show you its reasoning)
				1. Helps us understand why the AI is suggesting certain strategies or ideas (we are seeing its thought process or reasoning)
	2. By explaining why each element is important, the process becomes clearer and more logical
	3. Sam can use this method to understand the key components of his product launch and why they matter, leading to a well-reasoned and comprehensive plan
	4. Flow:
	
	```
	Input -> Thought -> ... -> Thought -> Output
	```
	
		1. Purpose: Guide the AI to think through the problem clearly
			1. We can make ChatGPT to walk through the logic step by step
		2. Best use cases: Great for strategy, planning, and problem-solving
		
	5. Example:
		1. Write a product launch plan for a new line of smart home security cameras. Using chain of thought, start by outlining the key components of a launch plan, then explaining why each component is essential, and finally providing examples of best practices.
			1. It provides a logical breakdown of product launch plan
			2. Helps us understand **why** for each part of the AI's output and its reasoning
6. **Building Smarter Responses**
	1. Reasoning models take a similar approach to frameworks like Chain of Thought and Tree of Thought.
	2. These models pause before responding, taking the time to think logically and structure their output. This results in more well-reasoned, higher-quality responses even when the prompt is simple.
	3. Don't worry if you're not familiar with reasoning models just yet - they'll be explained in more depth later.
	4. Just know that the frameworks you're learning here are the building blocks behind some of the smartest AI tools out there.
	5. **Tree of thought Reasoning**
		1. Here, multiple ideas are explored before settling on the best option.
		2. It's perfect for tasks that require creative exploration and evaluating various possibilities.
		3. In Sam's scenario, this can be used to brainstorm different strategies for his launch, giving him a range of ideas to refine before choosing the most suitable one.
		4. Flow:
		
		```
		Input
			Thought 		... 				Thought
				Thought			... Thought			Thought		
				Thought			... Thought			Thought
				...
						Output
		```
		
			1. Purpose: Explore multiple paths, then choose the best.
			2. Best use cases: Perfect for brainstorming and strategic decisions.
	6. Example:
		1. **Write a product launch plan for a new line of smart home security cameras. Using Tree of thought reasoning, brainstorm several key strategies for the product launch (influencer marketing, outbound marketing, paid ads, content marketing, etc.), then evaluate which strategy would be best for a small business launching its first product.**
7. **Ask Before Answering**
	1. With Ask Before Answering, ChatGPT asks clarifying questions before giving a response
	2. For example:
		1. **Before responding, ask me any clarifying questions about the target market, the product features, or any specific launch goals to better understand what I want**
	3. Sam uses this to ensure the AI fully understands the context that's relevant for the instructions provided
	4. This helps make the final plan more relevant and tailored to the business's needs.
8. **Self-Critique Prompting**
	1. In this method, the AI model critiques its own work after generating an output, making improvements for clarity, structure, and relevance.
		1. The AI will generate an initial version, and self evaluate, improving the draft by
			1. Refining the language
			2. Adding missing detail
	2. For example:
		1. **Write a product launch plan for smart home security cameras. Then, critique the plan for clarity, structure, and relevance, and suggest improvements.**
	3. For Sam's product launch plan, he can ask the AI to write a draft and then critique it for clarity, structure, and relevance.
	4. This is a great way to get a polished and high-quality result before finalizing it.
		1. The output is polished and effective
9. **Decision Matrix Generation**
	1. A decision matrix helps compare multiple options across different criteria
	2. For example:
		1. **Construct a decision matrix comparing three marketing strategies (social media, email campaigns, and influencer partnerships) for a smart home security camera product launch. Compare them based on cost, read, and potential ROI**
	3. Which can be valuable in Sam's use case in order to evaluate different marketing strategies, comparing factors like cost, reach, and potential ROI
	4. This framework provides a visual comparison, helping users make a more informed decision based on multiple factors
10. **Reverse Prompt Engineering**
	1. This approach involves analyzing a finished output to figure out which prompt generated it.
	2. For example:
		1. **Here is the product launch plan for the smart home security cameras. Reverse engineer it to come up with the most likely prompt to have created this plan.**
	3. Allows our Avatar to work backward from a successful product launch plan, understanding the key elements of the prompt that led to the output.
		1. Helps understand:
			1. How to improve
				1. Helps troubleshoot and refine prompts
			2. How to repeat similar results

		1. Words
		2. Sequences of words

		2. me find
		3. find a remote
		4. a remote marketing job
		5. market

		7. Me
		8. ...

	4. As we add input to the prompt, model updates it predictions. Taking extra context and data that was added

	#	probs		next token ID		predicted next token

		2. It contains

			2. Background information

			1. Can result in responses being incomplete despite providing the right information

					1. run - running

		1. It helps the model to figure context out by weighing how important each surrounding word is when it is trying to understand the context of the sentence

### Behind AI Image Models and How They Work ###
1. Topics:
	1. How do the models construct images?
	2. What types of models exist?
2. How AI Generates Images:
	1. Trained on millions of images: AI learns patterns, colors, shapes, styles
	2. Builds from scratch, pixel by pixel - no templates, no cut-and-paste
		1. It predicts the color, light, and form of the overall image from scratch
	3. Starts with static noise, gradually forms a complete, vivid picture
3. GANs: The Creative Duel
	1. Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)
	2. Generator: Produces fake images designed to look real
	3. Discriminator: tests each image, spotting fakes vs. real
		1. Shared with the generator
	4. A continuous feedback loop that sharpens both - like an endless art battle
	5. Structure:
	
	```
	Generator (produces synthetic data) -> Discriminator (evaluates and distinguishes data) -> Adversarial process (continuous improvement of generator) -> Generator
	```
	
		1. The loop sharpens the skills on both sides
		2. Sometimes the generator gets stuck generating the same types of images
			1. Mode collapse
4. VAEs (Variational Auto Encoders): Compress, Imagine, Rebuild
	1. Encodes data (complex images) into latent space - an abstract, compressed version (simpler forms)
		1. They understand and reimagine the data they are trained on
	2. Samples points from that space to construct new, realistic images (or rebuild the image from compressed data)
		1. It is like capturing the essence of something and remixing it
	3. Great for generating smooth, controlled variations - never just copies
		1. Faces
		2. New product designs
		3. They explore possibilities within learned patterns
			1. VAE takes a specific spot in the latent space and starts to rebuild from there
			2. The sample point moves through the decoder (like unrolling the scroll) and outputs reconstructed image
		4. It imagines and is guided by what it has learned
5. Diffusion Models: From Noise to Art (Stable Difusion, Midjourney)
	1. AI learns both to destroy (add noise) and rebuild (remove noise)
		1. For regenerating images
	2. Begins with pure static and sculpts it into clear, detailed images
		1. For completely new images
	3. Excels at producing high-quality, diverse outputs - but slower than GANs
	4. Working principle:
	
	```
	x0 (original image)
	| add noise
	v
	x1 (slight noise)
	| add noise
	v
	xT (pure noise)
	| Start Reverse
	v
	xT (pure noise)
	| Denoise
	v
	xT-2 (even less noisy)
	| Denoise
	v
	x'0 (reconstructed image)
	```
	
		1. It is very effective
		2. Slower and demands high computing power
		3. Very good at generating brand new images from scratch
6. How Text Beomes an Image
	1. AI decodes your words: turns prompts into color, shape, and mood
	2. Clearer, more detailed prompts = sharper and more accurate images
	3. Think of it like a chef: your prompt is the recipe for the final dish
		1. Example: If you describe in vivid detail such as soft brush tone, pastal vibes, calm sea, etc, you will get a more accurate image of what you have in your mind.
7. Differences:
	1. GANs - Speed painter
		1. Works fast contructing something convincing even if it is rough around the edges.
	2. VAEs - Like an architect
		1. They sketch out a blueprint from the image and then they fill in the details to bring it to life
	3. Diffusion - Like sculptors
		1. They start from a block of noise and they carve in away little by little until the vision appears fully formed
8. How to choose?
	1. Prompt will set the direction
		1. AI will do the heavylifting, predicting, and refining and shaping your idea into something brand new.

	1. This prompt engineering doc is designed to **optimize** your ideation process and enhance your output quality. The document comprises **two distinct sections**: a simplified section and an advanced section. The **simplified** section is ideal for generating _quick_ideas_ and obtaining concise answers with minimal effort (for quick prompting). One the other hand, the **advanced** section delves into the intricacies of _crafting_detailed_prompts_ that yield comprehensive outputs suitable for personal and business purposes.
3. What is Prompt Engineering
	1. Prompt engineering is the process of **designing** and **optimizing** prompts used in natural language processing (NLP) models, such as chatbots or virtual assistants. This involves crafting prompts that are clear, concise, and effective in eliciting the desired response. (Aka prompt engineering is like _making_an_effective_fishing_lure_ - just as a well-designed lure is more likely to catch the fish you want, a **well-crafted prompt** is more likely _to_elicit_the_desired_response_)
4. 3 Principles of Prompt Engineering
	1. **Be specific**: The more criteria you give, the more _focused_ the output will be.
	2. **Work in steps**: Break tasks _into_small_chunks_. This returns better results, just as you would with a human.
		1. Example: Don't ask ChatGPT to write an entire book
			1. Start with table of contents
			2. Go into each chapter
			3. Go into each concept
			4. ...
	3. **Iterate and improve**: _Re-work_ the inputs and have ChatGPT improve on it's _own_output_.
5. What Makes a Good Prompt
	1. Great outputs all come down to the **data** the model was trained on, its **parameters**, and good **prompting**. Since _we_can_only_control_one_of_these_, here's what good prompting boils down to:
		1. **Clear and concise** language that is direct and unambiguous.
		2. The **persona** that you assign to ChatGPT aka _who_it_will_be_acting_ as in your prompt.
			1. A mathematician
			2. A biologist
			3. A psychologist
		3. The **information** and **examples** that you provide aka your **input**. (information and examples can be referenced by ChatGPT in the previous chat history)
		4. A specific **task** that you are requesting ChatGPT to complete aka your desired **output**
		5. **Refinement** as needed once you receive your first response aka reiteration until receiving the desired output
6. Main Prompting Steps
	1. **Define the problem or goal**: Clearly _articulate_what_you_want_ChatGPT_to_help_you_with_ and what kind of response you are looking for.
	2. **Use relevant keywords and phrases**: Input the most useful industry and _topic-related_terms_into_the_prompt_, which will help guide ChatGPT toward generating the desired response
	3. **Write the prompt**: Craft a concise prompt that clearly communicates the **information** and **task** that is required, using the keywords and phrases identified in step 1 and 2.
	4. **Test, evaluate, and iterate**: Generate responses with ChatGPT, evaluate the results, and adjust the prompt as necessary to elicit the desired response. Repeat the process until a successful prompt is created that _consistenty_generates_the_desired_response_.

### Prompt Priming ###
1. Prompt Priming
	1. "Priming" refers to the practice of providing some _initial_input_ to the model **before** generating a response. The initial input is intended to guide the model toward generating a response that is _more_relevant_and_coherent_ with the user's intended output. (**You can put entire pieces of text you've written for ChatGPT to be trained on**).
		1. We can paste all past work from the last few months, and ChatGPT will understand how to replicate your speaking style, tone, grammar, and words that I use.
2. Example 1:

```
Without priming: Where should I go on my next vacation?

With priming: I'd like to go on a trip with _my_wife_and_kids_, the _location_should_be_tropical_, we'd love to be _close_to_the_beach_, I'd like the flight to be one _direct_flight_ to and from LAX, and I have a travel _budget_of_$5,000_. Where should I go on my next vacation?

Priming Output: One suggestion for your tropical family vacation within a $5,000 budget could be Cancun, Mexico. Direct flights from LAX are available, and you can find affordable all-inclusive packages with prices starting at around $1,000 per person for a week-long stay. Popular family activities in Cancun include snorkeling, visiting ancient ruins, and exploring local beaches and restaurants.
```

3. Exmaple 2:

```
Without priming: Please construct 3 potential titles for my new online course that teaches how to use AI.

With priming: Please construct 3 potential titles for my new online course that teaches how to use AI. Here is an _example_of_my_recent_course_titles_, please _emulate_the_styling_and_written_format_ of these: "Video Editing Masterclass: Edit Your Videos Like a Pro!" "Cinamatography Materclass: The Complete Videography Guide"

Priming Output:
1. AI Mastery: The Complete Guide to Using Artificial Intelligence
2. The AI Expert: Learn to Harness the Power of Artificial Intelligence
3. AI Foundations: A Step-by-Step Guide to Using Artificial Intelligence
```
